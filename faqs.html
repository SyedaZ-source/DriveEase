Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common car rental questions.

How can I book a car?

Open Available Cars, select a car, and click Book This Car. Then fill in the booking form.

How is the rental price calculated?

The total rent is calculated by multiplying the daily rental price by the number of rental days.

Can I choose different cars?

Yes, you can explore the available cars and select the car that suits your needs.

Can I change my booking dates?

This demo does not support changing a submitted booking. Please contact the rental service for assistance.

Does the website accept online payments?

Online payment is not available in this version of the website.
